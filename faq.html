What is the average temperature in Iceland during October?


Average Daily Temperature: Hovering around 4°C to 5°C (39°F to 41°F).
Precipitation: October is one of the wettest months, averaging 80-98 mm of rain.
Wind: Strong, chilly winds are highly common and can make the temperature feel significantly colder.
Daylight: Decreases rapidly from about 11 hours at the start of the month to just 8 hours by Halloween.

Key items to pack when coming to Iceland?


Winter jacket, warm socks, warm hat/balaclava, hiking boots/winter boots, thermal layers, camera, passport.

Should you transfer currency to Icelandic Króna?


You do not need to transfer or exchange currency before your trip. Iceland is essentially a cashless society.
If you prefer to carry a small amount of cash as a backup, simply withdraw Icelandic Króna from an ATM upon arrival at Keflavík International Airport (KEF)
